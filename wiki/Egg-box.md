# Egg box

The game hands you a number of randomized eggs (1 to 90).

## Filters

- **Legendary Pokemon:** allowed, none, or only legendaries.
- **Evolution line length:** only Pokemon whose family has 1, 2 or 3 stages (none chosen means any).
- **Only Pokemon that do not evolve from another** (the way eggs normally are).
- **Allow the same species twice.**

The Pokemon are picked after everything else is randomized, so the filters use the evolution lines and legendary status of the randomized game. The same seed gives the same eggs.

## Where they come from

A program that edits a game file cannot write to your save, so it cannot put eggs in your boxes. Instead the game itself hands them over. In **Emerald (English)** the egg woman of Lavaridge Town, who normally gives one Wynaut egg, gives all of them in one conversation. The game puts them in your party while there is room and then in your PC boxes, starting at the box that is open. The woman also no longer refuses when your party is full.

Keep room: if every box is full, an egg is lost. That is why the maximum is 90 (three boxes).

## Other games

Crystal, HeartGold, SoulSilver and Black 2 can only hand over what fits in the party, and sending the rest to the PC needs a code patch for each game. Generation 6 and 7 have no script tooling in this project yet. These are planned.
