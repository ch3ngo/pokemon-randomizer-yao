# Shops

## Extra items

Add up to 64 items to every regular Poke Mart, each with a price (for example Rare Candy for 10, Ability Capsule for 5000). They are added after any shop randomization, so they are never shuffled away. Items the game does not have are skipped, and prices are adjusted to what each game can store:

- Generation 1: up to 999999.
- Generation 2 to 4: 1 to 65535.
- Generation 5 to 7: multiples of 10 (a requested 1 becomes 10).

Games whose shops cannot grow (Generation 6 and 7) put the new items in place of the least important ones, never everyday supplies. Everything that was adjusted or skipped is listed in the log under "Custom shop items".

The original "Add cheap Rare Candies" option still exists and ignores this list.

## What shop randomization changes

Shuffle and Random never touch the regular Poke Marts (the progression shops every town has, and the TM shops) in any generation, so Poke Balls, potions, repels and the rest always stay where they are (rule set on 12/10/2026). Only the special shops are changed: the secondary shops with balls and mail, department store floors, herb, incense, vitamin and X item shops, and similar. Every game has some (Emerald and Omega Ruby have vitamin, herb and X item shops, FireRed has the Celadon Department Store floors, Platinum and SoulSilver have the department stores and the secondary shops). The "guarantee evolution items / X items" options place those items in those special shops, as many as fit.

## Protected items

You can also pick items that keep their original slots when the special shops are randomized or shuffled. Any item can be added.
