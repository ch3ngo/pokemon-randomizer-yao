# Softlock check

After randomizing, the program looks at the randomized data for problems known to block a playthrough and reports them in the log. It never changes anything.

Today it checks one family of problems: the field moves the game needs early on (the HMs of the first badges). If no Pokemon you can obtain, counting starters, wild, static and gift Pokemon and everything they evolve into, can learn one of them, the log says so by name. Try another seed, or turn on full HM compatibility.

More rules can be added over time.
