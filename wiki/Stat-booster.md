# Stat booster

Make weak Pokemon worth using.

- **Boost chosen Pokemon:** raise a Pokemon's base stat total by a percentage (1 to 300). The increase is spread over its stats in proportion, so a fast Pokemon stays fast, and no stat goes above 255.
- **Floor:** raise every Pokemon below a base stat total (for example 300) up to it.

Nothing is ever lowered. Shedinja keeps its 1 HP. The boost runs after the stat randomization, so it also applies to randomized stats. The log lists how many Pokemon were changed and some examples.
